Our Story

A Unique Fusion of Elegance & Creativity

At Habiba Beauty Salon and Art Gallery, we believe that beauty is an art form. Our space is more than just a salon; it's a sanctuary where premium beauty treatments meet inspiring artistic expression. Whether you're here for a transformative hair style or to admire custom art pieces, we provide an atmosphere that nurtures both your look and your soul.

Located in the heart of Sheikhupura, we offer a luxurious escape with professional care, advanced techniques, and a touch of artistic flair in everything we do.
