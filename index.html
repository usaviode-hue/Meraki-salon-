FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment by calling us at +92 322 4080586, visiting our salon, or using the contact form on our website. We recommend booking in advance, especially for bridal and special occasion services.

What are your operating hours?

We are open Monday to Sunday from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. For bridal appointments and special events, we offer flexible timing by prior arrangement.

Do you offer bridal packages?

Yes, we offer comprehensive bridal packages including makeup, hair styling, skincare treatments, and more. We provide personalized consultations to create custom packages based on your preferences and budget.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, bank transfers, and major digital payment methods. For bridal packages, we require a deposit to confirm your booking.

Do you use premium products?

Absolutely! We use only high-quality, international beauty brands and professional-grade products to ensure the best results for our clients.

Where are you located?

We are located at Paran Signature Studio, PARAN PLAZA, Near Children Hospital, Opposite Punjab College, Sheikhupura 39350, Pakistan. You can find us on Google Maps for easy navigation.

What is your cancellation policy?

We request at least 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. For bridal appointments, please refer to our booking agreement for specific terms.
