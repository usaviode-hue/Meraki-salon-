Frequently Asked Questions

Find absolute transparency about our bridal bookings, products, sterilization processes, and custom requirements.

Do you offer on-location makeup for out-of-city weddings?
Yes, Meraki Salon & Makeup Studio provides high-end on-location bridal makeup services across Lahore, Gujranwala, Faisalabad, and other major cities. This package includes custom styling, assistance, and requires prior booking and travel accommodation arrangements.
Which cosmetic brands do you use for bridal makeup?
We pride ourselves on using 100% genuine, premium international beauty lines. Our cosmetic kits consist strictly of luxury brands, including MAC, Huda Beauty, NARS, Estée Lauder, Charlotte Tilbury, Kryolan Professional, Anastasia Beverly Hills, and Dior.
How does Meraki ensure clinical-level hygiene?
Client safety is our paramount priority. All metallic manicure, cutting, and facial tools are scrubbed, disinfected, and sterilized inside an autoclave machine before sealing. Non-reusable tools like cotton pads, sponges, and buffer pads are strictly single-use.
Is prior consultation mandatory before hair transformations?
While not strictly mandatory, we highly recommend a complimentary 15-minute diagnostic session with our head colorist before coloring, balayage, or chemical treatments. This enables us to test your hair structural integrity and propose custom, non-damaging color solutions.
What is your cancellation or rescheduling policy?
We value our artists' and clients' time equally. For regular services, you can reschedule or cancel up to 24 hours in advance free of cost. For premium bridal packages, changes must be processed at least 7 days before the main event to secure deposit rollover.
